PAI-Actor: Cinematic Multi-Character Replacement in Dynamic Scenes

Bangxun Tang*, Heyuan Gao*, Yiren Song*, Guian Fang, Zijian He, Jie Yang, Mike Zheng Shou

合作：With Show Lab, NUS、 and Utopai Studios

arXivproject pagecode coming soon

替换真实电影片段中的多位演员，每位演员只需一张参考照片，同时保留原片的表演、背景、镜头运动和光照。

Replaces several actors in a real film clip, one reference photo per actor, while keeping the original performance, background, camera motion and lighting.

Data
PAI-Actor Dataset：从电影素材中构建的约 3 万个片段。PAI-Actor Dataset: about 30K clips built from film footage.
Teacher
双向扩散 Transformer，用于高质量 1080p 生成（片段约 4 秒）。A bidirectional diffusion transformer for high-quality 1080p generation (clips of about 4 s).
Student
把教师模型蒸馏成自回归模型（块因果注意力、KV 缓存、self-forcing），可生成 17 秒的 1080p 视频。The teacher distilled into an autoregressive model (block-causal attention, KV cache, self-forcing) that generates 17 s of 1080p video.
Results
在九种方法中误差（MSE、LPIPS）最低；16 人用户评测中，每一项的得票率都最高。Lowest error (MSE, LPIPS) among nine methods; largest vote share in every category of a 16-person user study.

PAI-Studio: Cinematic Video Background Replacement with Camera-Aware Motion

Heyuan Gao*, Bangxun Tang*, Yiren Song*, Guian Fang, Zijian He, Jie Yang, Mike Zheng Shou

合作：With Show Lab, NUS、 and Utopai Studios

arXivproject pagecode

根据一到三张参考照片，把在绿幕前拍摄的演员放进新场景：背景随镜头运动，演员的光照也随新场景调整。

Places an actor filmed on a green screen into a new scene from one to three reference photos: the background moves with the camera, and the actor is relit to match.

Data
CineStudio：来自电影和网络视频的 3 万个片段。CineStudio: 30K clips from films and online videos.
Model
上下文扩散 Transformer。复制时间位置编码，把每张参考照片固定到指定的输出帧；用光照扰动训练，让模型学会给演员重新打光。An in-context diffusion transformer. Cloned temporal positional encodings pin each reference photo to a chosen output frame; illumination-perturbation training teaches the model to relight the actor.
Results
所有指标都优于 VACE 和四个商业系统（Kling O1、Kling 3.0 Omni、Runway Aleph、SwitchX）；25 位评测者的两两对比中，相对 Kling O1 的胜率为 56.3%–63.6%。Best on every metric against VACE and four commercial systems (Kling O1, Kling 3.0 Omni, Runway Aleph, SwitchX); preferred over Kling O1 in 56.3–63.6% of comparisons by 25 raters.

Heyuan Gao*, Bangxun Tang*, Zeyu Gao

Image generators that place a product into a new scene often garble its small text and logos. FineForge post-trains FLUX.1 Kontext with GRPO and an edge-aware reward, raising OCR accuracy from 0.440 to 0.589.

Beyond Lip Sync: Reference-Grounded Oral Refinement for Audio-Driven Portrait Animation

Bangxun Tang

arXiv

现有的口型同步模型能保住整张脸，却只会画一张"平均"的嘴。RGOR 能画出这个人自己的嘴唇和牙齿。

Lip-sync models keep the face recognizable but draw a generic mouth. RGOR renders each person's own lips and teeth.

Data
NeRSemble 按人划分：290 人（2,900 段句子）用于训练，50 个没见过的人用于测试。NeRSemble split by person: 290 identities (2,900 sentence clips) for training, 50 unseen identities for testing.
Model
基于 LatentSync-1.6。每一帧都参考同一个人其他录像里的 16 帧，以及 4 张绕过 VAE 的高清嘴部图。Built on LatentSync-1.6. Each frame is conditioned on 16 frames of the same person from other recordings and on 4 HD mouth patches that bypass the VAE.
Training
成对判别器把生成的嘴和本人的参考图放在一起比较；反例里有别人的真实嘴型，所以"逼真但不是本人"的嘴会被惩罚。A paired judge compares the generated mouth with the person's own reference; its negatives include real mouths of other people, so a realistic but wrong mouth is penalized.
Results
在 8 个开源和商业系统（包括 Kling 和 HeyGen）中，嘴部 LPIPS（0.297）和 DISTS（0.163）最好；19 人评测的四个问题都得票最多。Best mouth-region LPIPS (0.297) and DISTS (0.163) among eight open-source and commercial systems, including Kling and HeyGen; most votes on all four questions of a 19-rater study.

REFIT: Recognize, Fix, and Test Wearable Sensor Placement Shifts without Labels

Bangxun Tang

arXiv

可穿戴传感器换了一种方式戴回去，固定不变的动作识别模型看到的就是"同一个动作，但坐标轴变了"。REFIT 从无标签数据中识别这种变化，在模型前面把它还原，并在没有标签的情况下检验结果。

When a wearable sensor is put back on differently, a frozen activity model sees the same motion on changed axes. REFIT identifies the change from unlabeled data, undoes it in front of the frozen model, and checks the result without labels.

Results
真实重新佩戴：准确率 0.547 → 0.955（最好的无标签基线为 0.827）。8 个数据集、116 位未参与训练的受试者：0.861，最好的基线为 0.384。Real re-attachment: accuracy 0.547 → 0.955 (best label-free baseline 0.827). Eight datasets, 116 held-out subjects: 0.861 vs 0.384 for the best baseline.
